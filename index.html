DeathApp privacy policy

Last updated 3 October 2026

The short version: your answers and your results never leave your phone. If you say yes, DeathApp sends anonymous usage statistics (for example, which questions get answered or skipped) so we can improve the app. If you say no, nothing is sent. You can change your mind at any time.

What the app stores

To work out your life expectancy, DeathApp asks about things like your date of birth, sex, country, habits and health. Your answers are saved only on your device, in the app’s private storage, so they’re still there next time you open it. The home screen widgets read a small summary of your estimate (expected date of death, birth date) from that same on-device storage.

What leaves your phone

Your answers and your results never do. DeathApp has no account, no ads and no crash reporting, and it works offline. We never receive your answers, so we can’t sell, share or lose them.

Anonymous usage statistics (optional)

When you first open the app, it asks whether it may send anonymous usage statistics. Nothing is sent unless you tap “Sure”. You can turn this on or off at any time under “How it works” → Privacy.

If you agree, the app sends these events to Mixpanel, the analytics service we use:

  • a question was answered or skipped (which question, its topic, and how many you’ve answered so far, but not your answer)
  • your life expectancy was shown for the first time (but not the number)
  • a story card was shared (which card design, not its content)

Each event also includes the app version, your device model and operating system version, and a random ID that the app creates. The ID isn’t linked to your name, email, phone number or advertising ID. We ask Mixpanel not to use your IP address to work out your location. “Start over” and turning the statistics off both delete the ID from your phone, so later data can’t be linked to earlier data.

We use this only to understand how the app is used and to improve it. We don’t sell it, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone else. Mixpanel processes it for us as a service provider and keeps it under its retention settings. Because the ID is random, we can’t tell which data came from you, but you can stop sending more at any time. If you’re in the EU or UK, the legal basis is your consent.

Sharing

If you tap Share, the app draws an image on your device and hands it to your phone’s share sheet. Where it goes from there is up to you and the app you choose.

Deleting your data

Use “Start over” in the app to erase your answers, or uninstall DeathApp, which removes everything it stored. Clearing the app’s storage in your phone’s settings does the same. To stop usage statistics, turn them off under “How it works” → Privacy.

Children

DeathApp is meant for adults and isn’t directed at children.

Not medical advice

DeathApp is for entertainment and general information only. It is not a medical device and doesn’t diagnose, treat or predict anything about any individual. If you’re worried about your health, talk to a doctor.
